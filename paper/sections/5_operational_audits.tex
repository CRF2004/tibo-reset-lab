\section{Operational and Application Audits}

The automation test suite exercises fresh and stale feeds, missing and unknown signals, duplicate
locks, off-schedule issuance, and append-only revision behavior. All eight predefined drills pass.
A Windows scheduler runs preflight, forecasting, and scoring at separate times. This arrangement
cannot guarantee that external services never fail, but it turns failures into visible logs and
missed-run records.

We separately audit whether users report that an announced reset was applied. Among 20 public
reports, 13 describe arrival without an explicit mechanism error, four describe arrival with a
window or hard-versus-banked mismatch, and three describe failure. Only one report has an exact
timestamp; it appears 32 minutes after its announcement. Evidence auditing grades one record A,
14 B, and five C. Reporter identities are not stably captured, so reports from the same discussion
cannot be treated as independent Bernoulli observations. These data identify mechanisms and
counterexamples, not a population failure rate.
